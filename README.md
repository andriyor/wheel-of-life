# Wheel of Life

**Try it:** https://wheel-of-life-psi-nine.vercel.app/

A calm Wheel of Life for anxious and ADHD minds, for anyone who keeps feeling "not enough" — at work, in relationships, at home or with money — and for people whose body or mind is struggling. Four short steps:

1. **Check in** — rate 9 areas 1–10, including Body and Mind & mood separately (sliders, or tap/drag the wheel). Tick *Not in my control right now* or *Good enough for this season* to let an area go.
   Tick *The “I'm not enough” voice is loud here* to see a kind reframe for that area and note one thing you did anyway, however small.
2. **What matters** — pick up to 3 core values; each one adds a ★ to the areas it feeds.
3. **Tradeoffs** — spread 10 energy points across the week. When they run out, the app offers to move one from the least important area.
4. **Focus** — at most 3 areas (gap × importance, skipping what you let go), each with tiny 5-minute steps to tick off.
   Everything you did anyway is gathered under *You did these anyway*.

**Low-energy mode:** tick *My body or mind has less to give right now* (illness, pain, depression, burnout). The weekly budget drops from 10 to 5 points, Focus shows at most 2 areas, and every step becomes rest-sized — doable from bed. If Body or Mind scores 3 or lower, Focus gently suggests talking to a doctor or therapist.

**Heavy season:** when the average score is 4 or lower, Focus shrinks to one area and its smallest step, and gently points to real support (someone you trust, a doctor, or a helpline via [findahelpline.com](https://findahelpline.com)). This is a self-help aid, not therapy.

Answers stay in your browser (localStorage).
